Consider another **hypothetical illustration**: 400 units of qualifying casino wagers with a 1.5% house edge. The theoretical house-edge amount is 400 multiplied by 0.015, or 6 units. Applying 3.5% to those 6 units gives 0.21 units of rakeback. Applying 3.5% directly to the 400-unit turnover would use the wrong base.
