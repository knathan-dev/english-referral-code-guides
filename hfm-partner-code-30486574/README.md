## Decide what “taking a break” means

A pause can mean leaving an account open without activity, moving funds out, opting out of a bonus or asking to archive the account. Those are different actions. Describe the intended action precisely when contacting support, especially if an open position or promotional balance remains.

Write down what you want to retain and what you want to end. For example: “I want to stop using this trading account, withdraw any eligible funds and understand the effect on its credit.” This is clearer than “delete my bonus,” which may leave the operator uncertain about the account, balance and permissions involved.

Do not make an unwanted trade simply to keep a promotion active. If a bonus requires account activity that no longer suits you, the appropriate comparison is between the terms and your preferences. A conditional credit is not an obligation to remain exposed to the market.

## Review inactivity before leaving the account unattended

The cited HF Markets (SV) document contains a 60-day inactivity provision for bonus-scheme accounts and describes termination and archiving consequences. Check its full wording with the operator for your account, including how any remaining eligible amounts would be treated. A similar number in another promotion should not be assumed to carry the same definition or consequences.

A calendar reminder can help you review the account before a planned extended absence. The reminder is for making an informed decision, not for generating artificial activity. If the account will remain unused, ask whether a formal opt-out or closure process is more appropriate and obtain an explanation of any remaining balance.

Keep the support answer with the accepted terms. If the offer changes during your break, that record can clarify what was discussed, though it cannot override the contract or guarantee that future conditions stay unchanged.

## Account for credit removal before a withdrawal

The reviewed Top-Up terms describe credit as nonwithdrawable margin and provide for proportional bonus removal when funds are withdrawn. They also require explicit consent when archiving an account whose remaining balance consists only of bonus funds; the document treats that removal as final. These are entity-specific provisions, not a universal description of every HFM account.

A practical check is to distinguish withdrawable funds from promotional credit and to ask what the account would show after your requested action. If positions remain open, changes in available margin may matter. Ask the operator to explain the effect before submitting a request you do not understand.

For illustration, a screen could show 300 units of personal funds and 100 units of credit. The presence of a combined figure does not prove that all 400 can be withdrawn. Use the account's actual balance classifications and current terms rather than adding every number together.

## A concise exit checklist

Before completing a pause, withdrawal or archiving request, make sure you can identify the correct trading account; the applicable promotion document; any open orders or positions; the amount the operator describes as withdrawable; and the treatment of remaining credit. These details reduce ambiguity without requiring you to disclose private information outside official support.

Retain the final confirmation after the requested process completes. A message acknowledging receipt of a request may not establish that the account has already been archived or that a withdrawal has settled.
