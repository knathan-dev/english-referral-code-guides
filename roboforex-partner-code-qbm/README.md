# RoboForex partner code qbm: daily rebates up to 90% of eligible partner commission

**RoboForex partner code qbm** is associated with **daily rebates of up to 90% of the publisher's eligible partner commission**, subject to the confirmed share and eligible account activity. That rebate is a separate partner arrangement from the broker's cashback services. If you first use a demo account, treat any simulated rebate as a learning example and verify the live partner payment independently.

The useful approach is to learn from the demo while keeping a separate set of questions for the real account. This guide explains that distinction without projecting simulated results into an earnings promise.

## What the demo rebate demonstrates

RoboForex's [official demo-account page](https://roboforex.com/forex-trading/trading/demo/) describes trading with virtual funds and virtual rebates through its Cashback service. That lets a user observe how a demonstration entry appears in the platform. It is not evidence that a cash withdrawal is available or that a particular partner has assigned a rebate share.

Use the entry to practice reading records. Identify its date, currency, account label and relationship to the surrounding activity. Can you find the same account in the platform and Members Area? Do you understand which figures are simulated? These operational questions are worthwhile even if you decide never to open a funded account.

Avoid treating a virtual credit as a validation certificate for qbm. It cannot establish the eligible commission generated by a real account, the publisher's agreed percentage or the payment schedule that will apply. A screenshot of the demo proves what the demonstration displayed, and little beyond that.

## Confirm the partner arrangement directly

If you choose to register and are eligible for the broker's services, use the [supplied qbm invitation](https://rinfinity.com/a/qbm). Preserve the lowercase spelling. Where partner information is visible during registration, inspect it before submitting. Ask the broker to confirm attribution if the account record does not make it clear.

Next, ask which live trading account and instruments are covered by the offered arrangement. The answer should define the actual rebate share and payment schedule, as well as any exclusions. Do not assume that one account's eligibility extends to every additional account you later create.

For an existing profile, ask what association is already recorded. Clicking a new invitation does not establish that the profile has moved to another partner. Avoid duplicate registrations to try to manufacture a new-user status.

## Give each percentage a named base

The offer here is up to 90% of eligible partner commission. The base is important. It is not 90% of the deposited amount, account turnover or trading loss, and it does not mean that every cost in a statement is reduced by 90%.

For illustration, suppose eligible partner commission for a period were 15 units and a confirmed arrangement returned 80%. The result would be 12 units. At a confirmed 90% share on that same base, it would be 13.50 units. These are hypothetical calculations that demonstrate the formula; neither number predicts an account's actual rebate.

Now suppose a demo shows a credit of 12 units. Matching the first example's number would still prove nothing about the real partner arrangement. The two figures could arise from different rules and different bases. A numerical coincidence is not a reliable reconciliation.

## Keep the broker service in its own column

The [official Cashback instructions](https://roboforex.com/help/faq/promotions/how-to-receive-cashback-rebates/) describe the broker's service, including its qualifying conditions and monthly account-history entries. That published service is a separate reference from the publisher-supplied daily commission-sharing offer. Check both sets of terms if your account is eligible for both.

A simple personal worksheet can use three headings: simulated entries, broker Cashback, and publisher partner rebate. Under each, record the source and the period. Do not add a virtual amount to real payments, and do not count one real credit twice merely because two descriptions appear to refer to it.

The worksheet is a way to organize evidence, not a forecast of recurring income. Leave unconfirmed amounts blank. A missing figure is more informative than inserting the highest advertised percentage without knowing its base.

## A decision point before funding

Before making a funding decision, you should be able to explain the chosen live account's ordinary costs independently of the rebate. Review the current account specifications and relevant product terms. If you cannot understand the account without assuming the maximum rebate, seek clarification before proceeding.

Also consider whether trading would still fit your plan if no rebate were paid for a particular activity. Costs and market losses can exceed any commission share returned. Increasing activity solely to generate a larger rebate can therefore make the overall outcome worse.

When asking support for help, send the account reference and a focused question through the appropriate authenticated channel. Keep passwords, security codes and identity documents out of public discussions of the offer.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is a demo rebate withdrawable?**

The demo page describes virtual rebates. A simulated credit is not a cash payment from the publisher.

**Does qbm automatically establish a 90% share for everyone?**

The supplied offer is a maximum. The actual rate and eligible commission must be confirmed for the arrangement and account.

**Should I combine a monthly broker credit with a daily rebate estimate?**

Keep the sources separate until each actual payment has been identified. An estimate is not a received credit.

**Can a demo still be useful?**

Yes. It can help you understand the platform and records without depositing real funds. Its value is learning, not proof of future earnings.
