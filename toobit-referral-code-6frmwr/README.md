The [official fee schedule](https://www.toobit.com/en-US/support/fee-rate) distinguishes markets and fee tiers. It also says the applicable tier is determined when an order executes. Read the current schedule for the exact market you intend to use and compare it with the fee information in your account. A demo screen is not a substitute for that check.

For illustration only, imagine an eligible transaction with a value of 1,000 units and a fee rate of 0.06%. Its fee would be 0.60 units. If a separately confirmed 20% reduction applied to that entire fee, the reduction would be 0.12 and the resulting fee 0.48. These numbers illustrate multiplication; they are not a quote for your account.

Now change the example to a transaction carrying no trading fee. A percentage reduction of zero still produces zero. Also, a trading-fee offer does not establish reductions in a transfer charge, funding payment or any other separately calculated cost. Ask which charge is eligible instead of applying one percentage to every balance movement.
