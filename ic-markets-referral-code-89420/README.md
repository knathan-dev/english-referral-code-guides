## Map the currencies before comparing numbers

Start with three labels: the currency held by your payment source, the base currency of the intended trading account, and the currency you expect to receive on a withdrawal. They may be the same, but do not assume that they are.

IC Markets Global's [Standard account page](https://www.icmarkets-global.com/en/trading-accounts/standard-account) lists several supported base currencies. Availability and the choices shown during registration should be checked for the actual entity and account you use. A currency listed on a global page does not establish that every payment method in every country supports it.

For each transition, ask whether a conversion occurs and who quotes the rate. A payment provider could convert before the broker receives the money, while another route could send the original currency for conversion later. The number displayed as “sent” can therefore differ from the amount that becomes available in the trading account.

## Compare the amount credited, with assumptions stated

Imagine two hypothetical routes for sending the same 1,000 units from a payment source. One quotation produces 980 units in the destination currency after all stated charges. Another shows 990 but leaves a further conversion unspecified. The second headline is not yet a complete comparison. You need the same destination currency and the full set of charges before drawing a conclusion.

These figures are illustrative, not current exchange rates or quotations. Their purpose is to show why comparing percentages alone can miss a fixed transfer charge or an additional conversion. Request a complete quote where the provider offers one, and note the time because conversion rates can change.

Also distinguish the cost of moving money from the cost of trading once it arrives. A bank or payment-provider charge is not a Raw Spread commission. Changing the trading account type may not remove a funding expense that occurs outside the broker.

## Transfers between accounts deserve a currency check too

The [official help centre](https://www.icmarkets-global.com/en/help-resources/help-centre) says transfers between trading accounts do not carry an internal transfer fee, while a transfer to an account in a different currency requires agreement to a conversion rate. That distinction illustrates why “no transfer fee” should not be interpreted as “no currency effect.”

Before an internal transfer, identify the source and destination accounts and read the quoted conversion where applicable. Consider whether the transfer serves an actual purpose. Moving money repeatedly between currencies simply to compare balances can create unnecessary uncertainty about the starting amount.

If positions remain open, understand how removing available funds could affect the account. A partner attribution does not replace ordinary account checks or make a transfer harmless to margin availability.

## Read the trading-cost terms on their own basis

IC Markets Global's [account overview](https://www.icmarkets-global.com/en/trading-accounts/overview) distinguishes Raw Spread and Standard pricing, including commission-based accounts. A spread starting at zero does not mean the account has no trading charges. Read the applicable platform, commission units and product specifications rather than importing figures from a different account.

The practical question is whether the complete arrangement suits your intended use: funding route, account currency, trading costs and withdrawal method. A referral code can identify the partner relationship, but it should not be credited with every ordinary feature of the selected account.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does 89420 provide free currency conversion?**

No such code-specific benefit is established here. Use the conversion quote and terms for the actual transaction.

**Should the account currency match the currency I already hold?**

That may simplify one part of the funding path, but the appropriate choice depends on available methods and how you expect to use the account. Compare the actual alternatives rather than assuming one currency is best for everyone.

**Can a transfer be free but still involve conversion?**

Yes. The official help centre describes that distinction for transfers between accounts with different currencies.

**Where should I resolve an unclear credited amount?**

Keep the payment confirmation and the account entry, then ask the relevant provider or authenticated broker support which rate and charges were applied.
