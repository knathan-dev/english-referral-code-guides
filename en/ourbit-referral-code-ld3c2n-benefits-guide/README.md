# OurBit referral code LD3C2N: up to 20% off eligible trading fees and the meaning of a zero-fee quote

Where an eligible trading charge is already zero, this percentage creates no additional saving on that charge. A zero commission also answers a narrower question than whether an entire transaction has no economic cost.

## Identify exactly what is described as free

A statement about a trading fee should be read as a statement about that charge. It does not automatically cover execution price, funding costs, withdrawals or every other aspect of account use. Name the category before deciding what a zero means.

OurBit's terms and official support material are relevant starting points for account and product conditions. Check the actual product and account context rather than extending a broad promotional phrase to every possible activity. Eligibility and pricing both need to match the use you intend.

## Keep the referral calculation honest at zero

If the applicable eligible fee is zero, a 20% reduction of that fee is also zero. There is no hidden positive amount to recover by multiplying a large transaction value by the referral percentage.

For a hypothetical positive eligible fee of nine units, the same confirmed percentage would save 1.80 and leave 7.20. These two examples can coexist within a cost analysis, but they should remain separate. Do not assign the positive-fee result to activity whose actual starting charge is zero.

## Examine costs that the commission line does not describe

A transaction can produce a different economic outcome because of the price and quantity executed. Moving assets can also involve costs described under separate rules. You need not invent a charge where none exists, but you should not assume every unexamined category is free.

Build a short list of the categories relevant to your intended action and mark each as confirmed, inapplicable or unresolved. This is more precise than adding a generic surcharge to every example or declaring the whole process costless based on one zero-fee line.

## Compare complete alternatives on the same basis

Suppose two hypothetical routes meet the same objective. One has zero explicit commission but a total acquisition cost of 505 units. Another has a 500-unit acquisition cost plus two units of commission. The second route has a higher commission and a lower total in this illustration.

This is not a claim about OurBit's prices or a recommendation for another route. It demonstrates why the comparison must include the same intended quantity and relevant cost categories. A zero in one column does not settle the complete decision.

## Do not manufacture paid activity to use the invitation

If your suitable planned activity has no eligible fee to reduce, there is no reason to seek a paid transaction merely to make the referral benefit visible. A promotion should follow an appropriate use case, not create one.

Likewise, do not use increased activity to justify a larger reported saving. The remaining cost and exposure may rise even while the discount total rises. Keep the original purpose and spending limits independent of whether the account produces a recognizable promotional adjustment.

## Reassess when the product or conditions change

A zero-fee observation belongs to the conditions under which it was made. If you change products or the applicable terms change, check the new starting charge and coverage. Do not carry either a zero or a positive rate into a new context without support.

Retain a brief dated note explaining what was confirmed. If a future fee appears unexpectedly, compare the relevant records and ask about that specific change. The useful question is which condition applies now, not whether a historical slogan should override the account's current terms.
