This guide tackles a practical problem: comparing fees when the starting rate, activity mix or review period changes, without attributing every difference to the referral.

## Record the starting conditions of a comparison

A useful comparison names the product, period and account fee rate. Bybit's official fee guidance directs users to their account's fee information and explains that applicable pricing can depend on the relevant account and product conditions.

If you compare two months without recording those starting conditions, a lower bill may have several explanations. You may have traded less, used a different product or paid a different underlying rate. The referral percentage is only one possible contributor, so avoid treating it as the automatic explanation for the whole change.

## Compare like activity before comparing totals

A total fee number answers how much was charged. It does not tell you whether the same activity became cheaper. For that second question, compare charges on a consistent basis, such as similar eligible transaction value under the same fee category.

Maker and taker classifications can affect the starting fee. The official order guidance explains why the order's execution matters. You do not need to choose one type simply to make a comparison work; you need to identify which category each completed execution actually belongs to before grouping the results.

## Keep changes in the middle of a period visible

Suppose an illustrative account incurs nine units of eligible fees under one set of conditions and thirteen under another. If a confirmed referral reduction applies at 20% to both portions, the savings are 1.80 and 2.60, totaling 4.40.

If only the first portion qualifies, the saving is 1.80, not 20% of twenty-two. This example shows why a single monthly multiplier can fail. The real question is which charges were eligible under the terms that applied when those charges arose.

## Separate percentages from percentage points

A relative reduction changes a starting rate proportionally. In a hypothetical example, reducing a 0.08% fee by 20% produces a 0.064% fee. It does not mean subtracting twenty percentage points from 0.08%.

This distinction also matters when reading a statement that shows rates and amounts together. The referral reduction may be expressed as a percentage of the fee, while the original trading rate is a percentage of transaction value. Label both bases explicitly so the two percentages cannot be confused.

## Investigate differences with a small evidence set

For an unexplained charge, collect the execution identifier, applicable product, fee category and displayed amount. Compare that record with the account fee information and the referral terms. This is usually more useful than sending a screenshot of an account-wide total with no context.

Keep unrelated balances and personal details out of material you share. Ask a narrow question: which starting rate was used, which portion qualified, and whether the displayed amount already includes the reduction. The answer should identify the accounting treatment, not merely repeat the promotional headline.

## Use the result to improve the next estimate

Once the periods are reconciled, keep separate assumptions for the activity categories you actually use. A weighted estimate based on those categories is more informative than assuming every execution receives the cheapest advertised starting rate.

Do not increase activity to reach a fee scenario that looks attractive on paper. The estimate exists to explain the cost of a suitable plan. Even a correctly calculated reduction does not remove market exposure, execution uncertainty or the possibility that an unsuitable trade costs much more than its commission.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a lower monthly fee prove the referral saved the difference?

No. Changes in activity and underlying rates can also lower the total. Compare the eligible charges on a consistent basis.

### Where should I check the actual rate?

Use the account fee information and current official Bybit fee guidance, together with the referral terms assigned to the account.

### Can every execution receive the same reduction?

Only where the applicable arrangement covers those executions. Product and eligibility boundaries must be checked rather than assumed.
