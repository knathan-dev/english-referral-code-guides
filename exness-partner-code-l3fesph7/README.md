A useful first message might read: “Please confirm whether my account is associated with partner code l3fesph7, which trading accounts are eligible for the offered rebate, the percentage applied to partner commission, and the payment schedule.” This asks for four defined facts without presuming the maximum rate applies.

Avoid starting with “Where is my 90%?” because that wording leaves the calculation base unclear. Ask whether the underlying commission was eligible, whether a rebate was calculated, and which record will show the result.

The operator can address account attribution and platform records. The publisher may need to clarify the particular commission-sharing promise. Keep those responsibilities clear: one answer should not be presented as confirmation of facts that only the other party controls.

## Put the percentage on the correct amount

Consider an arithmetic example, not a forecast. If eligible partner commission were 40 currency units and the agreed rebate share were 90%, the corresponding rebate would be 36 units. A different commission base or an agreed share below the maximum produces a different amount. The number of lots alone is insufficient to prove what should be paid.

This calculation also explains why a rebate should not become a trading target. Increasing activity creates additional costs and market exposure; the commission share only returns a portion of a separately defined amount. Evaluate the account and trading decision independently of the hoped-for rebate.
